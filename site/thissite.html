General Information


No AI was used in the making of this site. Nor, to the best of my knowledge, was it used in the creations of the assets within the site. This site was made completely by scratch, with no copied code except to link spotify songs or to link other people's site buttons. Everything on here I did by trial and error or by looking up tutorials, usually from stack overflow. Thank the G-ds for stack overflow.


Why This Site Exists


I love to code! Originally this site was made and hosted on Wix. The old site was only for my music. I wanted to create a more casual site to host more of my projects and also just be a personal space. Between Wix being super expensive and less customizable, I wasn't happy with the old site. When I started to learn more about the indie web, my decision was made. I re-taught myself basic HTML after years without writing in the language and made this. Right now it's very simple, as this site is new and I am basing it off the rather simplistic design of my old site while I build confidence. Eventually, it will be more fun and filled with personality! I'm so excited to see where this site takes me and what it becomes.
