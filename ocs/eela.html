General


Eela is a human artificer. She is a bit of a mad scientist type, learned what she knows from her dad. She typically seems a little like her head is out in space, with her main lucid moments being talking about her science or her dad. She has a cat, Giddy. Giddy is short for Armageddon, but Giddy is most definitely not going to cause the end of the world. Definitely. One of Eela's favorite things she has ever made is armor for Giddy to protect him from vicious attacks form local mice.


Backstory


I have very little backstory for her. I haven't gotten a chance to play her yet. In one campaugn that didn't get off the ground, she was stuck in a strange land without any memory of her past, and the central mystery of the campaign was that all the player characters were missing their memories. But I would love to play her in a campaign and flesh her out more in terms of backstory.
