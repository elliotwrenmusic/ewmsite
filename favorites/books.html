For now this will have to remain an unformatted list, and also a work in progress. I will make this pretty soon! Here are some of the books that made me who I am today. Warning in advance that some of these authors are homophobic or otherwise have done things I do not and never will condone (looking at you Orson Scott Card). Their place on this list is not an endorsement of the author but rather a recognition of things that have shaped me in ways that can never be undone. This list is not in any particular order, just the order in which they came into my head.

  • Songmaster - Orson Scott Card
  • Ender's Game (Author's Definitive Edition) - Orson Scott Card
  • Hunger Games Series - Suzanne Collins
  • Percy Jackson Series - Rick Riordan
  • The Secret Life Of Bees - Sue Monk Kidd
  • Wicked - Gregory Maguire
  • Bless Me Ultima - Rudolfo Anaya
  • Diary of a Young Girl - Anne Frank
