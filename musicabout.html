Elliot Wren is an indie-pop singer-songwriter born and raised in the Boston area. Elliot’s biggest songwriting influences are Taylor Swift, Halsey, Chappell Roan, Sabrina Carpenter, Ha Vay, & EMELINE. As a non-binary artist, Elliot strives to be a positive influence on the state of LGBT music and to never shy away from using their music to speak the truth. Elliot has established themselves as a respected independent artist, performing with other local artists. Their singles have received overwhelmingly positive praise from multiple publications for their raw lyrical style and expressive sound, including multi-national recognition for Time Bomb.

Their latest album, Nightgown, speaks on the journey through a long, hellish nightmare and the clawing struggle toward the surface. Nightgown explores how, even when the body escapes, the mind can feel trapped in a feeling of defeat, rather than of victory. This album digs into the state of utter conflict, both external and internal, that results from a living hell. Empowering, engaging, deeply personal, and with so much to offer, Elliot Wren is excited to share new music, bringing fans on an emotional journey!
