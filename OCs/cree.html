General


Cree is my first ever dnd character! She is a warlock tiefling. I have not gotten to play her very often, but every time I get to bring her into a one shot I seriously love it. She is very daring and takes a lot of risks, a personality I love to explore by playing her. Her patron changes every time I play her, but I love building her as someone who stays afloat by talking her way out of the trouble she finds herself in. High charisma and wisdom, good at deception and persuasion. She tries to never take things too seriously and always play things by the seat of her pants.


Patron


Cree doesn't remember the day she received her powers from her patron. She doesn't remember anything about that period of her life. All she knows is her patron saved her from something, she doesn't know what. The only clue she has about what happened is the tallies that appear on her when she uses her powers, tallies that mark the days since her patron saved her. She also has a vague memory of someone named Kyil, who was a mentor to her. Kyil disappeared sometime during this period. Cree now has no idea wether he is even still alive. Ever since her patron saved her, she has travelled around to do his bidding. To survive, she made money by gambling, though is it really gambling if she uses her patron's gifts to load the dice?
